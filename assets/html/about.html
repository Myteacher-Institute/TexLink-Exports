About Us

TextLink Exports is a trusted company providing high-quality fabrics and clothing material to clients across the globe.We focus on efficiency, reliablity, and long-term partnership.

Our Story

From our 10,000 square foot facility in India, our team of over 100 skilled professionals produces 21,000 to 25,000 pieces monthly. We offer a diverse range of products, including kaftans, robes, jackets, jumpsuits, and resort wear, with over 150 unique styles and 3,000 designs.

Established in 2015, TextLink Exports is a leading name in apparel manufacturing and export. Specializing in ready-made and customized garments, we bring creativity and quality to every piece we craft.

Our commitment to sustainability drives us to use azo-free garments and reduce our environmental footprint. With exports to over 32 countries, including the UK, US, Africa, and Australia, we pride ourselves on transparency, on-time delivery, and exceptional craftsmanship.

To connent local producers with international markets while maintaing world-class standards.To bring undertanding and relationship betwwen customers and manfactures.
